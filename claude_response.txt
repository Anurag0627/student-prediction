  Foundation: constants everything imports

     to grasp.

     train.py so you understand what an experiment
     produces.
  9. src/models/train.py — the heart of the project.
     run_experiment() assembles preprocessing →
     optional resampler → model into a leakage-safe

     strategy (none / class_weight /
